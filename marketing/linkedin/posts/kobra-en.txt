Your collections team calls between 9 and 6.

Your debtor also works between 9 and 6.

That alone explains a good share of the calls nobody picks up. But the hours aren't the expensive part. The ORDER is.

Almost every team calls highest balance first. It sounds logical. The problem is that the balance tells you nothing about whether that money is coming back.

A $50,000 debt from someone who won't pay is worth less, in practice, than three $5,000 debts from people who will.

Sorting by balance is sorting by what you WANT to collect.
Sorting by balance × probability of payment is sorting by what you WILL collect.

Two different lists. And you can build the second one tomorrow, with a spreadsheet and the history you already have.
